\emph{Permeable soil.} In the $f_{10}$ soil ($k_s=360\mmh$) the bias changes sign: the usual model gives
$\Icd=125$, 128 and 129~mm/h for $\delta=0.02$, 0.01 and 0.005, and the complete model 127, 134 and 137~mm/h, 2--6\%
more; with $\delta=0$ the usual model gives 131~mm/h, while the minimum FS of the complete model with 140~mm/h is
1.0015. The critical intensities are 0.35--0.38$k_s$. The surface also ponds in this soil: with 140~mm/h in model (i)
the first vertex ponds after 6.75~h, 87 surface vertices are ponded when the FS reaches 1.01 (13~h) and the air
pressure reaches 40~kPa. The different behaviour of this soil is therefore not explained by the absence of ponding;
the contributions of the air and of the coupling were not separated for it.

\emph{Low-permeability soil.} In the $f_{100}$ soil ($k_s=3.6\mmh$) no 24-h rainfall of up to 80~mm/h brings a
20-m, $45^\circ$ slope to failure in either model; the complete model gives a minimum FS 0.08--0.11 lower (1.172
against 1.252 with 80~mm/h), and its minimum occurs after the rainfall, 30--36~h after its start, whereas that of
model (iii) occurs at 48~h, the end of the simulation. On the $63.4^\circ$ slope model (iii) stays 9--10\% above
failure at any intensity, whereas model (i) comes within 0.013--0.015 of one (Section~\ref{sec:ar}).

\emph{Bishop parameter.} With $\chi=S_e$ (for the Fredlund--Xing curve with $C(\psi)=1$, $S_e=S_w$) the suction
contributes less to the initial strength (initial FS 1.895 instead of 2.119) and the air pressure also enters the
effective stress, through $(1-\chi)p_a$. The critical intensity is 47--49~mm/h in model (iii) and 27--30~mm/h in
model (i) ($\delta=0.005$--0.02): the usual model overestimates it by 64--77\%, more than with $\chi=1$ (40--59\%).
With $\delta=0$ model (iii) gives 50~mm/h; the minimum FS of model (i) with 30~mm/h is 1.003, and its run with
36~mm/h stopped at the first FS $\le1.01$.

\emph{Confinement of the air.} Opening the base and the lateral boundaries to the air changes the minimum FS by less
than 0.002 (Section~\ref{sec:confinamento}).

\emph{Mesh.} With elements of 0.35~m instead of 0.75~m near the surface (7732 triangles instead of 1684; initial FS
2.106 instead of 2.119), the minimum FS of model (i) with 30~mm/h falls from 1.011 to 1.005 and that of model (iii)
with 48~mm/h from 1.010 to 0.987 (Fig.~\ref{fig:malha}). With the local slope of the minimum FS against the intensity,
these differences shift the critical intensities $I_{c,0.01}$ to about 30 and 46~mm/h; their ratio, 1.5, does not
change.
